Sample studies for the "Load Sample Studies" demo button.

Both are real, full multi-slice studies from The Cancer Imaging Archive (TCIA),
redistributed by Saga IT (https://saga-it.com/dicom/samples) under CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). See each folder's CITATION.txt
for the full required attribution.

- ct-chest-nlst/       Chest CT, ~150 axial slices — NLST Lung Cancer Screening Trial
- mr-brain-upenn-gbm/  Brain MR, ~64 slices — UPENN-GBM Glioblastoma Study

These are de-identified research datasets, not real patients seen at this practice.
